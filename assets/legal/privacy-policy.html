Privacy Policy

RPG: Real Person Game (“RPG”, “the app”)

DRAFT – not yet reviewed. This page describes how the app handles data today, written from the code. It must be reviewed and approved by the app owner (contact details, legal wording, jurisdiction) before it is used as the public privacy policy for a Google Play release.

What stays on your device

Your profile, quests, training history, character progress and settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. RPG has no account system and no RPG server that receives this data.

Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, RPG asks permission to read three kinds of data: steps, distance, and exercise sessions (activity type, start and end time, and distance recorded during the session).

  • Why: to show your daily movement, advance in-game Journeys, and add workouts recorded in other apps to your Training history.
  • Read-only: RPG never writes data to Health Connect.
  • Where it goes: this health data is stored on your device inside the app. It is not sent to an RPG server, sold, or shared with advertisers or other third parties.
  • Your control: you can revoke access at any time in Health Connect. In RPG you can remove imported data under Settings → Connections.

Strava (when available)

If Strava connection is offered and you choose to connect it, RPG reads your Strava activities (read-only) and stores the resulting workouts on your device. The Strava access token is stored on your device. Disconnecting deletes it and asks Strava to revoke access.

Other services the app contacts

  • Weather: your saved location coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the forecast.
  • Maps and routes: map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, and route waypoints you draw may be sent to routing.openstreetmap.de to calculate a walking route. Map libraries are loaded from unpkg.com.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

These services receive standard request information (such as your IP address) and are governed by their own policies.
